OUR MISSION

Beyond Pixels.

Empowering organisations to transform Earth observation data into transparent, actionable intelligence through human-centred AI, technological independence, and trustworthy analytics.

01

Outpacing Others

We accelerate geospatial image analysis through interactive AI. SATIFY combines semi-supervised learning with direct user interaction, allowing analysts to guide and refine geospatial models during the analysis process. Instead of relying on lengthy training cycles, models can be adapted and fine-tuned in real time, enabling significantly faster segmentation, object detection, and feature extraction while maintaining expert oversight and control.

02

Beyond RGB: See More with Every Band

Leverage the full spectral richness of your imagery, not just RGB. Exploit subtle spectral signatures across multiple bands to distinguish classes more reliably and create more accurate annotations.

03

Independent European Technology

We are committed to technological sovereignty and long-term independence. SATIFY is developed entirely in-house as a European software solution, free from external licensing constraints and vendor lock-in. Organisations retain full control over their workflows, data, and deployments, enabling secure and sustainable use in research, industry, and public-sector applications.

04

Explainable and Trustworthy AI

Critical decisions require transparent evidence, not black-box predictions. SATIFY provides explainable analytics that make the reasoning behind model outputs understandable and traceable. Our platform can generate reports documenting decision pathways, confidence metrics, and analytical assumptions, enabling informed decision-making in high-impact domains such as environmental monitoring, infrastructure planning, security, and public policy.

YOUR IMAGERY. YOUR WORKSPACE.

The SATIFY app. Give your imagery meaning.

Work with satellite, drone, and aerial imagery using the tool that fits each task. Fill a model-suggested region, fine-tune the model in real time to improve its predictions, label similar pixels, or draw the details yourself. Our adaptive, model-assisted workflow helps you label and analyse complex areas in fewer steps, reducing repetitive manual work while maintaining full control over every label. Spend less time annotating and more time reviewing and refining the results.

  • Semi-Supervised Learning

    Try Smart Fill and refine the model on the fly.

    Interact directly with AI-assisted segmentation, provide feedback, and fine-tune the model in real time to continuously improve predictions as you work.

  • Old but Gold

    Classic tools for precise annotation.

    Use traditional image annotation tools such as the pen, bucket fill, and polygons to label exactly what you want, down to the smallest detail.

  • Easy View

    Reveal features through multiple image representations.

    Switch seamlessly between normalisations, spectral bands, and false-colour composites to uncover patterns and features that may be difficult to identify in standard imagery.

  • Local or Global

    Choose your reach.

    With either fill tool, use Local to target a single connected region or Global to identify and label similar areas across the entire image.

  • One-Click Refinement

    Enhance label quality with classical image analysis.

    Apply proven image processing techniques with a single click to smooth boundaries, remove artefacts, and improve the accuracy and consistency of your annotations.
