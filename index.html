YOUR IMAGERY. YOUR WORKSPACE.

The SATIFY app. Give your imagery meaning.

Work with satellite, drone, and aerial imagery using the tool that fits each task. Fill a model-suggested region, label similar pixels, or draw the details yourself. Our model-assisted workflow helps you label complex areas in fewer steps, saving time on repetitive manual work. Spend more of your time reviewing and refining the details—with control over every label.

  • Smart Fill: put model masks to work. Choose a model-provided mask and click a region to apply your selected label. Switch masks to find the level of detail that fits.
  • Bucket Fill: follow similar colours. Fill pixels with colours similar to the point you select. Adjust the tolerance to include a narrower or broader range.
  • Pen: take care of the details. Paint labels directly onto the image, adjusting the brush size for broad strokes or precise corrections.
  • Local or Global: choose your reach. With either fill tool, use Local for one connected area or Global for matching areas across the image.
