LESS REPETITION. MORE DISCOVERY.

Three ways to move your work forward.

See the tools at work.
Three short demos. Play at your own pace.

01 / Smart Fill

Choose the region. Choose the reach.

Preview suggested regions, then choose how far your labels reach. Fill one connected area with Local, matching pixels in the current view, or matching pixels across the whole image.

  1. Compare suggested regions over the forest.
  2. Move from Local to In viewport and Global.
  3. Zoom out to review the labels beyond the original view.

The dashed outline shows the original editor viewport.

02 / Time series & change detection

Turn change into labels.

Compare imagery of the same place across three dates. Switch to change detection, narrow the range of NDVI change, and apply the selected pixels as labels to the current image.

  1. Compare the landscape with a shared view across dates.
  2. Select a range of NDVI decrease between August and September.
  3. Apply the selection as Open ground labels and review the result.

A selected range is a starting point for review, not a ground-truth land-cover classification.

03 / Pen & Fill gaps

Make every detail count.

Trace a boundary with the Pen, then fill the area inside it. Change brush size for the tight bends and use Fill gaps to paint unlabelled pixels while preserving your existing labels.

  1. Draw the yellow boundary with two brush sizes.
  2. Choose Water and switch to a wider brush.
  3. Fill the blue interior without overwriting the yellow outline.

Precise manual tools complement model-assisted labelling.

THE DETAILS ARE STILL YOURS

Smart assistance. Hands-on control.

  • Semi-Supervised Learning

    Try Smart Fill and refine the model on the fly.

    Interact directly with AI-assisted segmentation, provide feedback, and fine-tune the model in real time to continuously improve predictions as you work.

  • Old but Gold

    Classic tools for precise annotation.

    Use traditional image annotation tools such as the pen, bucket fill, and polygons to label exactly what you want, down to the smallest detail.

  • Easy View

    Reveal features through multiple image representations.

    Switch seamlessly between normalisations, spectral bands, and false-colour composites to uncover patterns and features that may be difficult to identify in standard imagery.

  • Local or Global

    Choose your reach.

    With either fill tool, use Local to target a single connected region or Global to identify and label similar areas across the entire image.

  • One-Click Refinement

    Enhance label quality with classical image analysis.

    Apply proven image processing techniques with a single click to smooth boundaries, remove artefacts, and improve the accuracy and consistency of your annotations.
