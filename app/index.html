LESS REPETITION. MORE DISCOVERY.

Less repetition. More possibility.

See the tools at work.
Five short demos. Play at your own pace.

01 / Smart Fill

A few clicks. A landscape labelled.

Choose how far Smart Fill reaches. Local fills one connected region, Viewport fills matching regions in your current view, and Global finds matching regions across the whole image.

  1. Compare all three modes from the same starting point.
  2. Zoom out to see where Viewport stops.
  3. See Global label matching areas beyond that boundary.

02 / Rivers & roads pen

Follow the bend.

Guide a broad stroke along a river or road. SATIFY detects the line inside it and fits its width to the imagery. Review the result, then add it to your labels.

  1. Quickly mark the area with a wide brush.
  2. Review the automatically detected path and width.
  3. Apply the line as a label.

03 / Sync labelling

One action. Several dates.

Label water and agricultural fields across dates in a shared view. With Sync labelling, Smart Fill uses each image’s own data to find the region around your chosen point.

  1. Label Water across the three dates.
  2. Switch to Agriculture and select the fields.
  3. Review both classes on each date.

04 / Change detection

See the change. Make it a label.

Compare two dates, explore their index difference, and select the change you want to label. Adjust the range while you review the result on the maps.

  1. Compare the same place across two dates.
  2. Select a range of vegetation-index decrease.
  3. Turn the selected change into labels.

An index change is a starting point for interpretation. Review the imagery to decide what it means.

05 / Propagate labels Experimental

Teach it a little. Label a lot.

Provide examples of your classes and let the tool predict unlabelled areas. Adjust the confidence threshold to leave uncertain pixels for your review.

  1. Draw Forest, Water and Built-up examples on a clean image.
  2. Preview predictions for the unlabelled areas.
  3. Choose a confidence threshold and apply.

Experimental · Review predictions before applying.

THE DETAILS ARE STILL YOURS

Smart assistance. Hands-on control.

  • Regions ready to review

    Preview, label, and refine with Smart Fill.

    Use generated regions to speed up annotation, then refine the labels with the tools and classes that fit your project.

  • Old but Gold

    Classic tools for precise annotation.

    Use traditional image annotation tools such as the pen, bucket fill, and polygons to label exactly what you want, down to the smallest detail.

  • Easy View

    Reveal features through multiple image representations.

    Switch seamlessly between normalisations, spectral bands, and false-colour composites to uncover patterns and features that may be difficult to identify in standard imagery.

  • Local or Global

    Choose your reach.

    With either fill tool, use Local to target a single connected region or Global to identify and label similar areas across the entire image.

  • One-Click Refinement

    Enhance label quality with classical image analysis.

    Apply proven image processing techniques with a single click to smooth boundaries, remove artefacts, and improve the accuracy and consistency of your annotations.
